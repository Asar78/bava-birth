I am really sorry to you...

Unna hate pannanum nu illa. Nejama unna kashtapaduthanum nu edhuvum pannala. I always want to see your smile and happiness. So adhukaaga naan enna venaalum pannuven.

Enakku therinja unmai...

Naan neraiya unna oru pakkam romba love pannalum, another side naan unna kashtapaduthuren-nu enakku theriyala. Naan ovvoru vaatiyum en pain unakku theriyanum-nu dhaan appadi pannuven; unna hurt pannanum-nu naan oru thadava kooda yosichadhu kooda illa.

Ego-aa?

So nee en mela “ego” apdinu oru label ippo vara otti irukka. Normal-aa love irukkura edathula ego irukkaadhu. Appadi paatha, naan unna love panna alavukku yaarunae panna mudiyadhu. Then epdi enkitta ego irukkum?

Unakku munnadi...

Unakku munnadi edhuvumae enakku perusa theriyadhu. Appadi paakumbodhu ego mattum epdi enakku theriyum? Enakku kovam varum, but unkitta ego kaatta maatten. Kovam dhaan kaatuven.

Sorry, Bava 🤍

Idhuvaraikum naan unakku panna hurts ellathukkum sorry. Nejama nee nethu morning enkitta pesuna tone-um pesunadhum romba hurt aachu. But anyway, naan ellarukkum sorry. Naan edhuvum venum-nu pannala. From your side, inda maari chinna chinna paasam edhirpaakuren. Adhu kedaikkala-nu enakku kovam varradhu thappu illa dhaana? Adha kaattuna nee un kovatha enkitta kaatura. Anyway, you are my Bava 🤍 Naan ketupen... nee pesu. Love you...
